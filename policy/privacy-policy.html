Welcome to FRALEN CRM. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and disclose information when you use the FRALEN CRM website, application and related services.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you create an account or use FRALEN CRM.

1.1 Account Information

When you create an FRALEN CRM account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password authentication information
  • Shop or business name
  • Contact information
  • Account and subscription information

Passwords are handled through our authentication provider and are not stored by FRALEN CRM in plain-text form.

1.2 Business Information

Depending on the features you use, FRALEN CRM may store information entered by you or your authorized staff, including:

  • Product information
  • Frame and lens information
  • Contact lens information
  • Sunglasses and accessories information
  • Customer information
  • Customer contact details
  • Orders and sales records
  • Purchase records
  • Payment and balance information
  • Inventory information
  • Staff information
  • Business reports
  • GST-related business information
  • Eye-testing reminders and related business records

1.3 Payment and Subscription Information

FRALEN CRM may use Razorpay or other authorized payment service providers to process subscription payments.

We may receive information such as:

  • Subscription ID
  • Payment status
  • Payment date
  • Subscription plan
  • Billing amount
  • Payment or transaction reference
  • Subscription renewal or cancellation status

1.4 Technical Information

When you access FRALEN CRM, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • General usage information
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Date and time of access
Section 02

How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your FRALEN CRM account
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide CRM functionality
  • Store and manage your business records
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Maintain subscription status
  • Process automatic subscription renewals
  • Send important service-related communications
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Maintain and improve FRALEN CRM
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Maintain service security
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information as a product to third parties.

Section 03

Customer and Business Data

FRALEN CRM is designed for optical retailers and businesses.

If you enter information about your customers, employees or other individuals into FRALEN CRM, you are responsible for having the appropriate authority, consent or lawful basis to collect and use that information.

You should not enter information into FRALEN CRM that you are not legally permitted to collect or process.

Section 04

Firebase and Cloud Services

FRALEN CRM uses services provided by Google Firebase for functions such as authentication, database storage and application infrastructure.

Depending on the feature being used, information may be processed through Firebase services including authentication and Firestore.

Access to your shop's business data is designed around your authenticated account. FRALEN CRM uses account-based data separation to help prevent one customer's business data from being accessible to another customer.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Infrastructure Provider

Google Firebase may process account and application data as necessary to provide the services used by FRALEN CRM.

Section 05

Payment Processing and Razorpay

Paid FRALEN CRM plans may use Razorpay for subscription and payment processing.

When you subscribe, Razorpay may process payment information and recurring-payment authorization according to Razorpay's applicable terms, privacy policy and security practices.

FRALEN CRM may receive subscription-related information from Razorpay, including payment status, subscription status, subscription identifiers and billing events.

We do not intentionally store your complete debit or credit card number, CVV or other sensitive card credentials on our own servers. Payment details are processed by the applicable payment service provider.

Automatic recurring payments require the applicable authorization or mandate from the customer. If a recurring payment fails, is cancelled or is otherwise unavailable, your FRALEN CRM subscription status may be updated accordingly.

Section 06

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

  • Firebase authentication
  • Authenticated access controls
  • User-specific database access
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Server-side processing for sensitive payment operations
  • Restricted access to administrative systems
  • Security monitoring and technical controls

Despite these measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Section 07

Data Sharing

We may share or allow access to information with service providers that help us operate FRALEN CRM, including providers for:

  • Authentication
  • Database and cloud infrastructure
  • Payment processing
  • Email or communication services
  • Security and technical operations

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or security, investigate fraud or abuse, or enforce our terms and policies.

We do not sell customer or business information for advertising purposes.

Section 08

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide FRALEN CRM services
  • Maintain your account
  • Maintain business records
  • Process subscriptions and billing
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal, accounting or regulatory requirements

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Section 09

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your FRALEN CRM account and associated information.

Account deletion may be subject to verification of account ownership, outstanding legal or financial obligations, required retention of certain records, fraud or security investigations, and applicable legal requirements.

Some information may therefore need to be retained for a legally required or reasonably necessary period.

To request account deletion, contact FRALEN CRM using the contact information provided on our website.

Section 10

Cookies and Similar Technologies

FRALEN CRM may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Remember user preferences
  • Improve functionality
  • Maintain security
  • Understand service usage
  • Diagnose technical issues

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some FRALEN CRM functionality.

Section 11

Third-Party Services

FRALEN CRM may contain integrations or links to third-party services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that we do not control.

Relevant service providers may include Firebase/Google and Razorpay.

Section 12

Children's Privacy

FRALEN CRM is intended for businesses and professional users.

FRALEN CRM is not intended for children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating FRALEN CRM accounts.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Section 13

International Data Processing

Some service providers used by FRALEN CRM may process information on servers or infrastructure located outside India.

Information may therefore be processed in jurisdictions other than the country in which you are located, subject to applicable laws and the relevant service provider's policies.

Section 14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in:

  • FRALEN CRM features
  • Payment and subscription services
  • Security practices
  • Applicable laws
  • Third-party service providers
  • Business operations

When we make significant changes, we may update the “Last Updated” date and provide additional notice where appropriate.

Section 15

Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your FRALEN CRM login credentials confidential
  • Using an appropriate password
  • Restricting access to your account and devices
  • Entering accurate information
  • Ensuring that customer information is collected and processed lawfully
  • Obtaining any required consent or authorization from customers or staff
  • Informing FRALEN CRM promptly if you suspect unauthorized access

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, account deletion or privacy-related requests, please contact FRALEN CRM through the contact information provided on the official FRALEN CRM website.
